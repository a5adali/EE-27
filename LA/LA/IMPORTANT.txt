Cramer's rule:
Pretty simple, for x1, replace col 1 with entries of B, and call that matix A1.
then x1 = |A1|/|A| => det(A1)/det(A)
same applies for any x_n

- upper triangle, all elements below main diagonal = 0,
vice versa for lower triangle
-> determinant of any triangle matrix is product of all elements in the diagonal 

- Square matrix in row-echelon form is upper triangle

- Diagonal matrix => both upper and lower triangle, e.g. I

Inverse of A = 1/det(A) * Adj(A)
-> note: Adj(A) = Transpose of cofactor matrix 
e.g. | C11 C21 | ^ T
       | C21 C22 |
